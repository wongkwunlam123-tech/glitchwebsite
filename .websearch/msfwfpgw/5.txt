1. Basic Vocabulary for Educational Innovation
To discuss educational innovation in English, it's essential to master relevant vocabulary. Here are some key terms:
Educational Innovation : Refers to the introduction of new methods, technologies, or ideas to improve education. 
Example:  "Educational innovation is crucial for adapting to the needs of the 21st century."
Pedagogy : The art or science of teaching and educational methods. 
Example:  "Innovative pedagogy can engage students more effectively."
Technology Integration : The use of technology to enhance learning. 
Example:  "Technology integration in classrooms supports personalized learning."
Flipped Classroom : A model where students learn content at home and engage in activities in class. 
Example:  "The flipped classroom model encourages active learning."
Blended Learning : A combination of online and traditional face-to-face instruction. 
Example:  "Blended learning offers flexibility and accessibility to students."
2. Common Phrases and Expressions
When discussing educational innovation, these phrases can help you express ideas clearly:
"To revolutionize education..." : Used to describe a major change or breakthrough. 
Example:  "Online platforms have the potential to revolutionize education."
"To bridge the gap..." : Refers to reducing differences or disparities. 
Example:  "Educational innovation aims to bridge the gap between theory and practice."
"To foster creativity..." : Encourages original thinking and problem-solving. 
Example:  "Project-based learning fosters creativity among students."
"To empower learners..." : Gives students control over their learning process. 
Example:  "Self-paced modules empower learners to take ownership of their education."
"To adapt to changing needs..." : Adjusts methods to meet new requirements or trends. 
Example:  "Schools must adapt to changing needs by embracing innovation."
