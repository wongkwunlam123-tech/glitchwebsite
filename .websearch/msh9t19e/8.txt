SMART CERTIFICATION
Learners who earn the base five micro-certifications are eligible to earn the SMART Certification. The SMART Certification is endorsed by the ARM Institute, Carnegie Mellon Robotics Academy, and the National Robotics Engineering Center. 
Pre-Apprenticeship to Apprenticeship
The SMART Robotics Technician Curriculum is also a registered pre-apprenticeship program in Pennsylvania. This pre-apprenticeship is tied to:  New Century Careers’ Robotics Technician Apprenticeship Program
Are you a student and are looking to go through this program?
The pre-apprenticeship program is currently being set up in various locations in Pittsburgh, PA. Please check back for updates or email us at  cmra@nrec.ri.cmu.edu . 
Looking to implement this program in your organization/school?
If you are an educational institution within Pennsylvania and are interested in implementing this program, contact us at  cmra@nrec.ri.cmu.edu . There may be grant opportunities available.
For all areas:  We have an  Implementer's Guide  that contains the overview of the program, course descriptions, links to needed materials, and some expectations.
