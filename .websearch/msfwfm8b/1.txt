A light rail train runs on the first day of its operation in Astana, Kazakhstan, May 16, 2026. [Kazakhstan's presidential press service/Handout via Xinhua] 
When the first phase of Astana's light rail transit system opened on May 16, Kazakh President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev took a ride on the new line and expressed thanks to the Chinese government and Chinese builders in Chinese, quoting the ancient proverb, "A journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step".
"I felt very proud," said Ji Chenglong, executive manager of the Astana light rail transit project at China Construction Sixth Engineering Bureau Co. 
History
Ji arrived in Astana with his team in July 2023. Working through winter temperatures as low as — 40 C, they adapted materials and construction methods to local conditions and overcame the challenges of building an urban rail system in extreme cold.
The project applied Chinese standards and technologies across design, construction, equipment and operations, including fully automated self-driving trains and intelligent dispatching.
Ji said that average daily ridership has exceeded 70,000, well above the design-stage projection of 25,000. During construction, the project created more than 2,000 local jobs, while the project team conducted ongoing technical exchanges and provided on-the-job training tailored to the project's needs, he added.
"Seeing more and more residents enjoy the convenience brought by the light rail has deepened my understanding that infrastructure is not simply about steel and concrete. It is an important means of improving people's lives and promoting development," he said, adding that this is the true meaning of Belt and Road cooperation.
He said he still remembered that President Xi Jinping once said the ultimate goal of the Belt and Road Initiative is "to explore new ways for countries from near and far to achieve common development and to open up a 'path of happiness' that benefits the whole world".
The Astana light rail is a vivid example of that path taking shape in Central Asia, Ji said.
"From a light rail line to a modern city, and from engineering cooperation to closer bonds between peoples, Belt and Road cooperation is not only a path of connectivity, but also a path of common development, friendship and cooperation, and greater well-being for the people," Ji said.
Askar Umarov, head of the Central Communications Service under the Kazakh president, said he believes that Kazakhstan and China are at a critical stage of development and revitalization, and are companions on the path to modernization.
He said that China's achievements in economic development, digital transformation, technological modernization and the implementation of long-term national strategies offer practical insights for Kazakhstan.
China is an important strategic partner of Kazakhstan, and understanding the thinking behind China's policies has significance beyond academic research, he said.
